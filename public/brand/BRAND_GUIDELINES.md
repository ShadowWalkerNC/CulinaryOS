# CulinaryOS Brand Identity & Logo System

> **Concept:** *The Sovereign Flame & Plate*  
> **Brand Purpose:** The open operating system for restaurants — sovereign kitchen data, closed-loop economics (recipe → fire → waste/cost), humans on POS/KDS, agents on MCP.

---

## 1. Visual Metaphor & Symbolic Logic

1. **The Precision Hexagon (The Sovereign Plate / Station):**
   - Represents the restaurant's operational boundary, table station, and deterministic data contract.
   - Chamfered edges mirror culinary geometry, induction cooktops, and kitchen prep trays.
2. **The Living Flame (The Culinary Fire):**
   - The heart of the restaurant: fire, passion, kitchen execution, and live service speed.
   - Dual-wing geometry signifies the partnership between humans (line cooks/managers) and autonomous agents (MCP/OS).
3. **The Core White Spear (Operating Truth):**
   - High-temperature precision center symbolizing money-integrity, immutable audit logs, and zero-hallucination operational truth.
4. **The Peripheral Protocol Pins (Network Nodes):**
   - Six network coordinates marking real-time POS, KDS, Server, Inventory, MCP, and Database bus connectivity.

## 4. Usage Rules & Clear Space

- **Minimum Clear Space:** Maintain padding equal to half the height of the flame symbol around all perimeter boundaries.
- **Minimum Size:**
  - Digital Icon: `24×24px`
  - Horizontal Lockup: `120×30px`
- **Avoid:**
  - Never distort or stretch aspect ratios.
  - Never place the full-color mark on vibrant red or yellow backdrops without an obsidian hex container.
