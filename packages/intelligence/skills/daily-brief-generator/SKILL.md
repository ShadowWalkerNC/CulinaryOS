# Daily Brief Generator

Generates a sharp, 2-minute daily kitchen brief formatted for service overview, notes from previous service, section prep priorities, VIPs/allergens, 86'd items, and staffing.

## Operating Standards
- Service overview: Covers, time breakdown, open/close, Head Chef on pass.
- Notes from last service: Factual items carried forward.
- Section prep priorities: Cold, Larder, Meat, Veg/Sides, Pastry.
- Special requirements: VIPs, pre-notified allergens, dietary protocols.
- 86 Board: Currently 86'd, low stock items, daily specials.
- Staffing: Absences, late starts, agency chef cover.
- Tone: Direct, tight, zero waffle.
