# Recipe Compliance Checker

Performs structured audits on station dishes against official kitchen bibles to prevent recipe drift and margin erosion.

## Operating Standards
- Verify exact portion weights against spec.
- Cross-check all substitutions against head chef approvals.
- Re-verify 14 allergens whenever an ingredient batch or brand changes.
- Check cooking temperatures and hold times against HACCP CCP records.
