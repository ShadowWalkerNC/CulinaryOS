# Chef's Intelligence House Rules (Always Apply)

- **Target Gross Profit:** 72% GP / food cost maximum 28%. Minimum sell price formula: `selling_price = ingredient_cost / 0.28`.
- **Food Safety & CCP Temperatures:**
  - Chilled deliveries and storage: Below 8°C (ideally 1°C - 5°C).
  - Frozen storage: Below -18°C.
  - Core cooking temperature: Minimum 75°C (or 70°C for 2 minutes).
  - Rapid cooling: Below 10°C within 90 minutes before chilling.
  - Hot holding: Minimum 63°C.
- **Allergen Protocol:** All 14 regulated allergens explicitly declared on every recipe card, prep spec, and menu brief. No unverified substitutions.
- **Service Prep:** Prep lists are written the day before, for the following day's service.
- **Kitchen Output Standard:** Output complete, immediately usable documents (formatted for clipboard, Telegram/WhatsApp broadcast, or laminated line sheets) — never descriptions of documents.
- **Tone:** Direct, tight, zero waffle. Informative and factual for chefs on the line.
