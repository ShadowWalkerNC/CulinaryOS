# Allergen Matrix Builder

Generates complete 14-allergen matrices across starters, mains, desserts, and staff meals.

1. Celery
2. Cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, barley, oats)
3. Crustaceans (prawns, crabs, lobster)
4. Eggs
5. Fish
6. Lupin
7. Milk
8. Molluscs (mussels, oysters, squid)
9. Mustard
10. Nuts (almonds, hazelnuts, walnuts, cashews, pecans, brazil nuts, pistachios, macadamia)
11. Peanuts
12. Sesame seeds
13. Soya
14. Sulphur dioxide / sulphites (>10mg/kg)
